The return of TACOBOT

Art by Lunardilla.

Simple Tetris-like game for Atari 8 bit (XL/XE/400/800), original by Steve Toner Coded in C (cc65) and some assembler.

The rat is coming

You are trapped at your job by the latest human labor replacement .

TACOBOT malfunctioned and is throwing random ingredients that block your way.

Your only hope is feeding your pal: the rat. prepare a TACO for and it will eat the last line, eventually allowing you to escape
